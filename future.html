Future

Looking ahead, my short-term goal is straightforward: graduate from Iowa State in December 2027 with my marketing degree and advertising minor, and step into a full-time marketing role where I can keep building on the experience I've already started gathering. Between my internships at Able Aerospace and empirical Foods, I've gotten a taste of both structured, data-driven marketing and fast-moving, creative content work for social media. I want a career that lets me keep doing both, rather than choosing one lane permanently.

More specifically, I'm drawn to brand marketing and content strategy roles where I can own a brand's voice across multiple platforms, not just execute someone else's plan. My internship experience has shown me that I enjoy the early, creative stages of a marketing campaign as much as the analytics that come afterward. I'd like a role, whether at an agency, an in-house marketing team, or a growing brand, that gives me ownership over real campaigns rather than a narrow slice of one.

At the same time, my entrepreneurship background isn't something I plan to leave behind once I graduate. Running Sweet E's Bakery for six years taught me that I like building things, and I don't think that instinct goes away just because I'm working for someone else's brand first. Longer term, I'd like to stay open to launching something of my own again, whether that's a small business, a creative agency, or a marketing consultancy, once I've built enough real-world marketing experience and industry knowledge to do it well.

For now, my focus is on finishing strong at Iowa State, keeping my GPA up, staying involved with AfterDark, and landing a full-time marketing role that stretches me the way my internships have. Wherever that ends up, I want to keep working at the intersection of marketing, creativity, and entrepreneurship, the same three things that brought me to this field in the first place.
