Internships

My internship experience spans two very different industries, but both roles come down to the same core skill: building marketing programs that actually reach the right audience.

Marketing Intern — Able Aerospace, Mesa, AZ

As a Marketing Intern with Able Aerospace, I built the company's first customer email marketing program, creating segmented HubSpot automated workflows for prospective and existing customers to drive engagement and strengthen customer relationships. I also designed an automated email signature auditing system using Microsoft Forms and Excel to standardize customer-facing branding across the company, and I collaborated with the Quality team to develop workplace signage, producing print and digital assets that promote quality standards across the facility.

Social Media Intern — empirical Foods, Dakota Dunes, SD

As a Social Media Intern with empirical Foods, I create engaging social media content, including reels, graphics, and posts, for multiple brands across platforms, tailoring each piece to a diverse range of target audiences. That work has resulted in a 28% average engagement rate across platforms. I use editing software within the Adobe Creative Suite to produce polished digital content, manage content calendars for several accounts, and plan future marketing content well in advance.

Together, these two internships have given me a well-rounded view of marketing: one built around structured, data-driven systems like email automation and branding standards, and one built around fast-moving, creative content production for social media. Both experiences have sharpened the marketing and branding skills I plan to keep building throughout my career, and both have shown me how much I enjoy the mix of strategy and creativity that good marketing requires. I came away from each internship with a much better sense of how marketing decisions actually get made inside a real company, and with a stronger portfolio of real content and campaigns to show for it.
