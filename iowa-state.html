Iowa State

Iowa State University has shaped both what I study and how I spend my time outside the classroom. I'm currently a junior in the Ivy College of Business, pursuing a Bachelor of Science with a major in marketing and a minor in advertising, and I'm on track to graduate in December 2027 with a 3.98 GPA. The marketing program at Iowa State has given me a strong foundation in branding, consumer behavior, and campaign strategy, while the advertising minor has pushed me to think more critically about creativity and messaging.

Marketing Director — AfterDark

Outside of coursework, I've tried to apply that marketing and branding knowledge in real organizations on campus rather than just in the classroom. Since September 2025, I've served as Marketing Director for AfterDark, a student organization at Iowa State, where I maintain our social media platforms to promote, highlight, and recap events. I use Canva and CapCut to create graphics and videos for event promotion, collaborate with graphic designers on posters and handouts, and oversee a mix of digital and print marketing across every event we run.

Creative Committee — Dance Marathon

Before that, I spent a year on the Creative Committee for Dance Marathon, one of Iowa State's largest philanthropy events. In that role, I generated social media content to inform followers about upcoming events and boost overall outreach, and I designed engaging posts using Canva to keep our messaging consistent and on-brand. Our team's collective marketing and fundraising efforts contributed to a total of $145,162.28 raised for the University of Iowa Stead Family Children's Hospital.

Between coursework, AfterDark, and Dance Marathon, Iowa State has given me a place to practice real marketing and branding work while I'm still a student, not just learn about it in theory. Those experiences, on top of my GPA and coursework, are a big part of what I bring into every internship and project I take on.
