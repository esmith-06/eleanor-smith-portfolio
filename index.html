Hi, I'm Eleanor Smith.

I'm a junior at Iowa State studying marketing with a minor in advertising, and someone who's been building brands since I was twelve, starting with a bakery business I ran out of my own kitchen. These days that same instinct shows up in internships, campus leadership, and a growing list of real marketing work I've gotten to be a part of. I care about the parts of marketing where creativity and strategy meet, and this site is where I share what that's looked like so far.

I'm a junior at Iowa State University's Ivy College of Business, where I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in marketing with a minor in advertising. Marketing has been the thread running through nearly everything I've done, from running my own small bakery business as a middle schooler to interning with companies that are building out their marketing and branding programs for the first time.

My interest in marketing started early. At twelve, I taught myself how to bake and decorate custom cakes and cupcakes, then built a small local business, Sweet E's Bakery, around it. Running that business for six years taught me branding basics long before I had a name for any of it: how to promote a small brand on social media, how to keep customers coming back, and how to balance creativity with the practical side of running a company. That entrepreneurship experience still shapes how I think about marketing today, and it's a big part of why I'm drawn to this field in the first place.

Since coming to Iowa State, I've turned that early instinct into real, professional skills. As a Social Media Intern with empirical Foods, I create content, including reels, graphics, and posts, for multiple brands, tailoring each piece to a different audience and managing content calendars across platforms. That work has averaged a 28% engagement rate, teaching me a lot about what actually makes an audience pay attention. As a Marketing Intern with Able Aerospace, I built the company's first customer email program using segmented HubSpot workflows and supported their branding and signage needs across the facility, a reminder that good marketing shows up in plenty of places beyond social media alone.

On campus, I bring that same energy to my involvement. As Marketing Director for AfterDark, I manage our social media platforms and oversee event promotion, using tools like Canva and CapCut to produce marketing content and video. I also spent a year on the Creative Committee for Dance Marathon, designing social media content that helped our team contribute to a campus-wide fundraising total of over $145,000 for a children's hospital.

I'm drawn to the parts of marketing where creativity, branding, and content strategy meet, where a good idea also has to perform and hold up under real results. Take a look through the tabs above for more on my internships, my entrepreneurship background, my time at Iowa State, and where I hope my career goes from here.
