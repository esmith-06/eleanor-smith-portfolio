Hi, I'm Eleanor Smith.

I'm a junior at Iowa State studying marketing with a minor in advertising, and someone who's been building brands since I was twelve, starting with a bakery business I ran out of my own kitchen. These days that same instinct shows up in internships, campus leadership, and a growing list of real marketing work I've gotten to be a part of. I care about the parts of marketing where creativity and strategy meet, and this site is where I share what that's looked like so far.

I'm a junior at Iowa State University's Ivy College of Business, where I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in marketing with a minor in advertising. I'm on track to graduate in December 2027 and currently hold a 3.98 GPA.

My interest in marketing started early. At twelve, I taught myself how to bake and decorate custom cakes and cupcakes, then built a small local business, Sweet E's Bakery, around that work.

Since coming to Iowa State, I've turned that early instinct into real, professional skills. As a Social Media Intern with empirical Foods, I create content and help support the company's digital presence.

On campus, I bring that same energy to my involvement. As Marketing Director for AfterDark, I manage our social media platforms and oversee event promotion, using tools like Canva to help connect students with campus events.

I'm drawn to the parts of marketing where creativity, branding, and content strategy meet, where a good idea also has to perform and hold up under real results. Take a look through the tabs above for more on my internships, my entrepreneurship background, my time at Iowa State, and where I hope my career goes from here.
