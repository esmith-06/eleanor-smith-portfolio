Hi, I'm Eleanor Smith.

I'm a junior at Iowa State studying marketing with a minor in advertising, and someone who's been building brands since I was twelve, starting with a bakery business I ran out of my kitchen and turning that early instinct into a strong interest in marketing, branding, and entrepreneurship.

I'm a junior at Iowa State University's Ivy College of Business, where I'm pursuing a Bachelor of Science in marketing with a minor in advertising. My work has centered on building brands, creating digital content, and understanding how strategy and creativity work together in the real world.

My interest in marketing started early. At twelve, I taught myself how to bake and decorate custom cakes and cupcakes, then built a small local business, Sweet E's Bakery, from the ground up. That experience taught me how branding, customer experience, and consistent execution matter just as much as the product itself.

Since coming to Iowa State, I've turned that early instinct into real, professional skills. As a Social Media Intern with empirical Foods, I created content and campaigns that help several brands connect with their audiences across social media platforms. As a Marketing Intern at Able Aerospace, I strategically created the company's first customer email campaign and developed improved internal processes to strengthen the company's branding.

I also bring that same energy to my campus involvement, where I use marketing to support events, communities, and organizations. As Marketing Director for AfterDark, I manage our social media platforms and oversee event promotion, using tools like Canva and CapCut to create engaging promotional materials and maintain a consistent brand presence.

I'm drawn to the parts of marketing where creativity, branding, and content strategy meet, where a good idea also has to perform and hold up under real results. Take a look around my site to see more of my work, experiences, and goals.
